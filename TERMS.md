## What this is

A free break tracker with a sense of humor, run by Lara Mateo as a personal
project. It logs when you say you went outside, tells you how long it has been,
and looks up the weather and nearby parks. There is no account and nothing to pay.

## What it is not

It is not medical, mental-health or safety advice. If it tells you to go outside
and there is a thunderstorm, a heatwave or a bear, stay in. Weather and park data
come from public sources ([Open-Meteo](https://open-meteo.com/en/terms) and
[OpenStreetMap](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright)) and can be wrong, out of
date or missing.
